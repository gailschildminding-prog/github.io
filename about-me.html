A Little About Me

My name is Gail Stewart, and I am a passionate childminder with over 8 years of experience in providing quality care for children. I love nature and spending time outdoors with the children I care for embracing the great nature of the Poole District.

My Setting

Upton is a quiet, family-friendly area in Upton, Poole, just outside the town of Poole. It is known for its welcoming community, green spaces, and safe residential streets, making it an ideal place for children to grow and explore. Nearby attractions such as Upton Country Park provide beautiful outdoor spaces for walks, play, and nature activities, offering plenty of opportunities for children to learn and enjoy the outdoors.
